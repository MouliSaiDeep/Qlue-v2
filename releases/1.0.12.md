# Qlue 1.0.12

- The dashboard now shows available Android updates with a release-note preview and an **Update** button.
- Tap the dashboard bell to see feedback that’s generating, ready, or unavailable, along with available app updates. Open feedback directly from the inbox or choose **Load older** to see earlier activity.
- A dot on the bell indicates unread activity. Qlue saves read status on this device for each account.
- The dashboard, history, and resume lists handle loading and retries more smoothly. Interview animations update independently of the transcript.

Android version **1.0.12**, build **10012**.
